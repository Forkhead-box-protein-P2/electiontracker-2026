Chamber headlines stay in context

The original reference headlines depend on named-finalist, unresolved-seat and caucus assumptions. They are not unconditional control probabilities and receive no manufactured control Brier or log score. Independent election winners are not automatically assigned a caucus.

The record and the rules

Frozen forecast, honest denominators

The sole forecast was built October 30 and selected before outcomes on November 3 at 07:07:51 UTC. Model, polling and market updates have stopped. Results cannot change the forecast or select a more flattering date. All 506 contests remain visible: 435 House, 35 Senate (including Florida and Ohio specials), and 36 governors.

Matching binary events use Brier score (p − y)² and natural-log loss −ln(probability assigned to the observed outcome). A zero-probability miss has infinite log loss; no clipping conceals it. Favorite accuracy excludes exact 50/50 ties. The competitive subset is fixed at 20–80% Democratic win probability. Candidate multiclass scores use the sum of squared category errors and remain separate from binary scores.

Conditional cases, finality and sunset

Named-finalist and candidate-field estimates are scored only after their exact condition is independently evidenced. Failed conditions are recorded as coverage failures; missing odds are never replaced with zero. One-major-party scenarios require ballot and rule verification. Unresolved forecasts remain explicitly unscorable even after their outcomes are known.

AP race calls are provisional. A leader, runoff qualifier, 100% reporting or estimated vote percentage is not a final winner. NPR/AP certification flags are labeled as provider-reported; final completion also requires archived official-source checks for all 506 races, finished recounts/runoffs and explicit scoring or coverage dispositions. Once that final release is verified, scheduled updating ends.

Sources, revisions and model limitations

Aggregate results: Associated Press via NPR. Each race links to its state results and retains source update time, retrieval time and raw SHA-256 in the downloadable record. Calls can be revised. A failed refresh retains the last dated result and displays the failure. No market prices or publisher race ratings enter evaluation.

This research baseline failed its full historical consistency screen. New 2026 scores do not erase that record or establish precise chamber calibration. See the full methodology.
