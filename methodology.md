# ElectionTracker 2026 methodology

The current model, `national-consistent-single-correction-ensemble-v1`, is an owner-authorized all-office research baseline. House, Senate and governor means, predictive errors, shared factors, election-rule links and joint event simulations were rebuilt together. Current model identity and its independent release review are separate from the retained original native component report.

Eligible ordinary polled races use an office-specific convex blend of raw aggregated polls and chronological fundamentals, plus one regularized net intercept. The fixed intercept penalty represents four election cycles. Unpolled and source-protected routes retain explicit native or separate candidate-model mean fallbacks. Current Maine Senate receives the separately reviewed transfer; no comparable historical Maine margin labels were invented.

Zero-location uncertainty is fitted from earlier complete final-policy errors, including valid unpolled and native fallback errors. No second mean correction is added. Nonnegative national, office, state and independent variance components use fixed equal-election moment weights and one pseudo-election of shrinkage. Shared loading geometry is a modeling assumption. The model retains t5 tails, 20,000 aligned simulations, conditional candidate fields, unresolved finalist qualification and unknown independent caucuses. Senate majority means at least 51 Democratic caucus seats; House means at least 218. Governors have no national control event.

The completed research comparison covers 2018, 2020, 2022 and 2024 at 1, 7, 14 and 30 days. Before comparisons, the original native archive reproduces exactly. All arms then use the same outcome-free guarded event cohort. Native and predecessor scores are labeled comparators, rather than inherited accuracy claims for the new model. The explorer retains every failed consistency check. Multiple dates and simulation seeds reuse the same elections and do not create independent validation observations. Original source publication vintages remain incompletely certified, and no 2026 outcomes provide prospective validation.

Each exact daily profile from 1 through 30 is prepared before release. Source refreshes cannot silently refit or select a policy. Immutable prior releases and rollback evidence remain preserved. Forecast history is separated by policy identity. Scenario bounds represent unresolved fields or caucuses; they are not confidence intervals. Market prices remain separate observations and never enter this forecast. No betting or trading is performed.
